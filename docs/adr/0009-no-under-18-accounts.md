# ADR-0009: No under-18 accounts in v1

Date: 2026-08-31 · Status: Accepted

## Context

India, DPDP Act 2023 defines a child as anyone under 18 and requires verifiable
parental consent, with no legitimate-interest basis and penalties to ₹250 crore.

## Decision

No accounts for under-18s. Adults create and manage child profiles on the tree.

## Consequences

- Sidesteps verifiable parental consent entirely
- Children still appear in the family history, which is what matters

* Teenagers cannot participate directly, which some families will want
* Revisit only with proper legal advice
