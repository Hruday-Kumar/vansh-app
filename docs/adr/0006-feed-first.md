# ADR-0006: The app opens on the feed

Date: 2026-08-31 · Status: Accepted

## Context

The app opens on the family tree. A tree is an archive: it is built once and
then has no reason to be revisited, which is why tree apps have no retention.

## Decision

The app opens on the Pravāha feed. The tree stays a first-class tab.

## Consequences

- There is something new on open, every time
- The graph becomes infrastructure that makes the feed personal, rather than a
  destination in itself

* The tree loses top-level prominence, which is the correct trade
